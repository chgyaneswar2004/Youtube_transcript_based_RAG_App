# YouTube Test Videos for the RAG App

10 short videos (about 5-10 minutes) with 5 test questions each. Five are AI/ML topics and five are biology/health topics.

All ten entries now include a direct YouTube link. Caption availability is checked when `evaluation.py` runs; duration notes are informational and are not used by the evaluator.
